This folder is PRE-LOADED with the English Gen VII event Wonder Cards that are
redeemable on Pokemon Ultra Moon (from the ProjectPokemon Events Gallery) -
Marshadow, Zeraora, Meloetta, Magearna, Celebi, the 2018 Legends (Ultra Moon
side), shiny Tapus, Ash cap Pikachu, shiny Poipole/Zygarde/Mimikyu, and more.

HOW TO USE
1. Run Script 15 (15-event-pokemon.c) in PKSM. It injects these Wonder Cards
   into your Mystery Gift album.
2. Save in PKSM, boot Ultra Moon, and talk to the delivery girl in any Pokemon
   Center to receive each Pokemon. They arrive with their real, legal event
   data (correct OT / ID / date / ribbon).

NOTES
- The Mystery Gift album holds a limited number of cards. If there are more
  cards than free slots, Script 15 injects as many as fit and tells you how
  many are left - redeem those in-game, then run Script 15 again for the rest.
- You can add more files here: *.wc7full or *.wc7 (Wonder Cards -> album) or
  *.pk7 (event Pokemon dumps -> injected straight into your boxes).
- These are version-matched to Ultra Moon (e.g. you get Solgaleo, not Lunala).
  If you have Ultra Sun instead, swap in the US/SUS cards from the Events
  Gallery.
